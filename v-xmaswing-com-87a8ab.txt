 About XmaSwing 
 XmaSwing is a very special festival at a magical time of the year! 
 As the largest and oldest event at the Hep Cat Club in Augsburg, XmaSwing has become a true tradition and a highlight. 
 The festival offers inspiring workshops in Lindy Hop and Solo Jazz, welcoming dancers from near and far. Just before Christmas, the city of Augsburg is even more beautiful than usual, inviting you to stroll through its illuminated streets, enjoy mulled wine, and indulge in delicious treats at the Christmas market.
