 Welcome to Lakedance – four days of sailing, swimming, sunshine and friendship by day… and dancing ALL DAY AND ALL NIGHT LONG.
 This festival is all about SWING music. Lindy Hop at its heart — with Balboa, Blues, and a touch of Fusion woven in. If you love to dance and never seem to get enough, you’re in the right place.
 Last year was beautiful. This year will be as unforgettable. 
 For the first time, we have the Konzertmuschel right by the lake exclusively for all four days . That means music all day long. This is not just a festival – it’s a dance marathon by the lake. You can take a break to go sailing, join a skill-sharing session, or simply enjoy the lake — but if you’d rather keep dancing non-stop, you absolutely can.

 a word from our kind supporters:
 Our cross-border swing dance project brings together the vibrant German and Swiss swing communities at the lake. At its heart is the idea of connecting people across borders through music, dance, and real human encounters. We kicked things off in June 2025 with a big event in Konstanz, organized by the local swing scene. And we are doing it again now in 2026! By switching locations across the borders, we’re celebrating intercultural exchange, building lasting networks, and strengthening collaboration across the region. Our goal? To create lively spaces where people from all backgrounds feel welcome, experience cultural diversity, and simply have a great time together. Thanks to the financial support of the Interreg ABH program, we’re able to bring this colorful and inspiring project to life.
 BAnds and Music
 The Shirt Tail Stompers
 The Shirt Tail Stompers
 London&#8217;s top vintage band, the Shirt Tail Stompers are firmly established at home and internationally playing Harlem Swing and the Hot Jazz of the 1920s, 30s and 40s. Featuring music from composers such as Duke Ellington, Benny Goodman and Count Basie.
 The Killin Jivers
 The Killin Jivers
 Dirty, cheeky, and full of mischief – that was the jazz culture of the 1930s, and it still can be today, as The Killin’ Jivers prove with their current album “The Dream of Day and Night.” This jazz isn’t just meant to be listened to – it’s made for dancing and laughing along. Inspired by acts like Slim & Slam from jazz comedy and The Cats and the Fiddle from the world of vocal street jazz, The Killin’ Jivers bring jazz back to the show stage – and never take themselves too seriously in the process.
 Jivers (Argentinia)
 Jivers
 Jivers is a four-piece band with a specific Jazz repertoire: vocal Swing of the thirties and forties. Mainly inspired in bands like The Mills Brothers, they adapt original arrangements to their instrumentation. Other times they create their own arrangements for not so traditional songs emphasizing in a close three part harmony and imitating instruments with their voices. The rhythm section consists of a guitar and a snare drum complemented with a typical traditional 20s Jazz formation: a tuba, a banjo and a washboard. 
 Swing Kids
 Swing Kids
 Young, Talented, and Award-Winning 
 Since winning the prestigious Swiss Jazz Award in 2008 as Switzerland’s favorite jazz band, the Swing Kids have captured hearts at home and abroad. Their tours have taken them across America, Asia, and South America, earning international acclaim and impressing even the most seasoned jazz professionals.
 Tim Schicker
 Tim Schicker
 Tim Schicker: vocalist and guitarrist and bandleader. His boudless stage energy turns every performance in a celebration. This time we will celebrate together his blues tunes that will dazzle you in the early morning hours.

 Weekend vibes
 photo by Fabibi 
 Stay in the Groove – Party All Night 
 If you hate it when a party ends just as you’re really getting into it, this is your place. At Lakedance, the music keeps playing, the floor keeps moving, and the fun doesn’t stop — so you can dance, connect, and celebrate for as long as you like.
 Each night has its own playful theme to spark your imagination and get you moving. Let the colors, costumes, and vibes carry you onto the dance floor
 Thursday – Colors & Beach Vibes: 
Start the weekend in full color—with bright tones, summer lightness, and everything that evokes sunshine, beach, and good vibes.
 Friday – Sailors & Sea Breeze: 
Dive into a night of lakeside elegance and carefree summer spirit. Think maritime stripes, flowing linen, light scarves in the wind, sailor hats, golden details, and the freedom of the sea—in blue, white, and gold.
 Friday Late – Undress to Unimpress: 
As night falls, we invite you to a beloved tradition on Lake Constance: a shared nude swim under the stars.
 Saturday – Underwater World: 
Immerse yourself in a magical underwater world—with shimmering fabrics, deep blues and greens, glitter, and flowing shapes. Think turquoise, purple, and shimmering black.
 Sunday – Golden Glow: 
Warm, bright colors and a gentle closing moment—yellow, coral, orange, like a summer sunset by the lake.

 Venues
 Apollo Kreuzlingen
 Apollo Kreuzlingen
 Two different live-bands await you in this beautifully restored cinema originally built in 1932. It reopened its doors to host a variety of cultural events in 2022 and we are beyond pleased to be able to have our parties in such a stunning and special location!
 Konzertmuschel
 Konzertmuschel 
 Nestled in the heart of the Stadtgarten, the Konzertmuschel is an open-air stage that has been a beloved gathering spot for music and culture lovers for years. This iconic lakeside pavilion hosts a variety of concerts and performances from spring to autumn, offering a unique atmosphere where live music meets the stunning backdrop of Lake Constance. Managed by the Cultural Office of Konstanz, it remains a vibrant space for local artists, community events, and unforgettable evenings under the open sky.

 Contact us
 Your booking becomes binding only after we’ve received your payment. Please note: We do not offer refunds, even in case of illness or unexpected life surprises.
