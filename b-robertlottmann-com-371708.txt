 Bringing Acoustic Music Back
 After more than ten years of playing modern, mainstream, commercial country in pubs, line dance clubs, and festivals, I went back to my roots to
 play the real deal: genuine, honest, acoustic music. 
 [javascript:] 
 Some more wood on the campfire...
 I’ll play a bunch of songs I grew up with (some are country, and yeah, some just ain’t...) and in between songs I'll talk a bit. You can sing along or
 even dance to it if you feel like it. I’m pretty sure I’ll play some songs you haven’t heard in ages. So sit back, relax, and enjoy the show.

 Acoustic Living Room & Campfire Concerts
 A living room concert is a totally acoustic show right at your place. Your living room, garden, or even your garage. All you need are plenty of seats or pillows for everyone, some drinks and
 snacks, and good vibes. For extra ambience, add candles or a even a campfire!
