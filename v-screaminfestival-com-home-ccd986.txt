 SCREAMIN’ FESTIVAL 2027
 Screamin’ 50’s Rock &#038; Rhythm &#8211; Since 1999
 It isn&#8217;t just a festival. It isn&#8217;t just a holiday. 
 First and foremost, Screamin&#8217; is about the music. Every year we carefully curate a line-up that celebrates the many faces of Rock & Roll, Rockabilly, Classic Country, Rhythm & Blues and vintage sounds. Alongside legendary artists, we&#8217;re committed to presenting unique productions, exclusive collaborations and exciting new discoveries—many of which have taken their first international steps in Calella before going on to earn worldwide recognition. 
 Just as important, Screamin&#8217; is a place where you can simply be yourself. Dress the way you like, dance the way you feel and enjoy the week at your own pace. Whether you&#8217;re wearing a perfectly tailored drape suit, a vintage dress, your favourite band T-shirt, or just shorts and flip-flops after a day in the sun, you&#8217;ll fit right in. 
 Some people never leave the dance floor. Others are happy sitting back with a drink, enjoying the music and the company. Some come to dance every song, others to discover new bands, hunt for records, admire classic cars, learn to dance, or simply spend a week surrounded by people who share the same passion. There isn&#8217;t a right way to experience Screamin&#8217;—because everyone brings something different, and that&#8217;s exactly what makes it special. 
 From morning until the early hours, Rock & Roll is everywhere. And whenever you feel like taking a break, Calella offers beautiful Mediterranean beaches, cafés, restaurants and a relaxed atmosphere, all just a few steps away. They&#8217;re not the reason people come to Screamin&#8217;—but they certainly make the experience even more enjoyable. 
 Come for the music. Leave with memories, friendships and experiences that will stay with you forever.

 Screamin&#8217; Festival 2027 Is Coming!
 Following the tremendous success of Screamin&#8217; Festival 2026, we&#8217;re already working to bring you another unforgettable week of music, dancing and Rock & Roll! 
 From 7–13 June 2027 , Screamin&#8217; Festival returns to Calella, Barcelona, bringing together the very best of Rockabilly, Rhythm & Blues, Country, Doo Wop and vintage culture at one of Europe&#8217;s most iconic festivals. 
 2027 will also mark the 50th anniversary of Elvis Presley&#8217;s passing , a milestone that will be celebrated with a very special show and exclusive surprises created especially for Screamin&#8217; Festival. 
 Streets of Bakersfield – A Special Tribute to the California Country Sound 
 Celebrating: 90 Years of Merle Haggard · 70 Years of Buck Owens on Capitol · 60 Years of the Golden Bakersfield Era 
 A unique production celebrating the artists who shaped one of the most influential sounds in American music, performed by an outstanding cast of international musicians. 
 2027 will also celebrate the 70th anniversary of Carl Perkins&#8217; legendary Dance Album , one of the most celebrated Rockabilly albums of all time. This landmark anniversary will be honoured with a spectacular tribute celebrating the music and legacy of one of Rock & Roll&#8217;s true pioneers. 
 Expect an outstanding international line-up, bringing together artists from the USA, Canada, Australia, Mexico and across Europe —truly the United Nations of Rock & Roll. 
 And that&#8217;s just the beginning! Enjoy the legendary Mexi-Go Party, the Happy Hula Party, the flea market, the vinyl meet, dancing until the early hours, sunshine, pool parties, great friends and the unique atmosphere that has made Screamin&#8217; Festival one of Europe&#8217;s favourite destinations for lovers of authentic American roots music and vintage culture.
