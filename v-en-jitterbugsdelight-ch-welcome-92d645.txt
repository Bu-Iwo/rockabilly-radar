 It's all about that swing
 January 8–10, 2027
 REGISTER
 [https://en.jitterbugsdelight.ch/welcome/#welcome-passes] 
 Jitterbugs Delight is all about swing dancing. Our goal is to help you improve your swing dancing skills, at any tempo, to any type of swing music and no matter who you dance with. You will have classes in several swing dances and you are guaranteed to broaden your horizons.
 This workshop is for you if you
 want to improve your social dancing skills - at any pace and with anyone.
 want to learn different swing dance styles and improve your ability to mix them.
 want to learn from some of the best international teachers. 
 What is a jitterbug? 
 The meaning of jitterbug has changed again and again in the history of swing dance. At the end of the 1930s, a jitterbug was a "swing fan". Today, jitterbug is often used as a generic term for various swing dances. We understand a jitterbug to be a swing dancer who combines the various swing dances and improvises with them.
 The number of spots is very limited.

 LEVELS
 At Jitterbugs Delight we ask questions about your dance experience in different swing dances and your interests . This way we can form groups that fit together well and inspire you with different swing dances. 
 This year, all participants have 8.5-10.5 hours of courses: 
 1.5 hours Lindy 
 1.5 hours Collegiate Shag 
 1.5 hours Balboa 
 2 hours of free-choice classes - you decide what will help you progress
 2 Masterclasses before the parties - Open Level with special topics from our teachers.
 2 hours of solo jazz on Friday (optional) 
 Your level for Lindy, Balboa, and Shag can be completely different. 
 There are 6 levels and 7-8 groups per dance style to keep the group size small. Level 1 is for absolute beginners in Balboa and Shag and for beginners with previous experience in Lindy Hop. Level 6 is the highest level for ambitious dancers. 
 You can find more answers on the subject of levels in the FAQ [https://en.jitterbugsdelight.ch/faq] . Just write to us if you have any questions.
