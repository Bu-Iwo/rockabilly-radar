 BFF History 
 Boogie Feet Festival - BFF was created back in 2015 by Sondre Olsen-Bye, his father Jan Åge and their dance club Lesja-Dovre Danseteam. 
 The first edition was held in Dombås, Norway January 2016. 
 Sondre´s family and especially his mother Ellen, local friend Inge Simonsen & Tanya Georgiievska became very important members of the BFF Crew during the next editions. 
 7 BFF editions was held in Dombås with more than 25 different nations represented! 
 BFF is today known as the festival of Sondre & Tanya! And is recognised as one of the very best pure Boogie Woogie Dance festivals in the world.
