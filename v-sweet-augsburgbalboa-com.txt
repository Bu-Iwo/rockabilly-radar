 Sweet Bal Weekend 
 in Augsburg (Germany) 
 March 5 - 7, 202 7 
 Registration starts Wednesday, October 14, 1 p.m.!  
 Sweet Bal Weekend 202 7 in Augsburg – A Weekend of Slow and Fast Rhythms! 
 Join us in Augsburg for an unforgettable weekend dedicated to Balboa! Dive into Slow Balboa and Balboa workshops , tailored for intermediate and advanced dancers, and refine your technique with the guidance of our expert instructors: Anni & Gašper and Mel & Jo .  
 Of course, the fun doesn’t stop at the workshops – we’ll be partying all night long! On Saturday night, get ready for an amazing party with live music from The Stardust Syncopat o rs , playing the perfect blend of slow and fast tunes to keep you on your toes! 
 Let the warm and inviting atmosphere captivate you as you dance, learn, and celebrate with fellow Balboa lovers in one of Germany’s most beautiful cities. 
 Come join the fun – the dance floor is waiting for you!
