 About 
 A fresh wave of Rock&rsquo;n&rsquo;Roll from Bern, Switzerland &mdash; TOMMY ROMERO delivers high-energy shows driven by rhythm, attitude, and classic Rock&rsquo;n&rsquo;Roll spirit. Rooted in old-school sounds with a modern edge, his live shows are made to be unforgettable. 
 ​ 
 With 100+ concerts across Switzerland, Germany, and Austria, TOMMY ROMERO has built a strong live reputation. His album received a review in the UK Rock&rsquo;n&rsquo;Roll Magazine, and local radio stations have featured and supported his music. He writes his own songs, collaborates with other musicians and keeps Rock&rsquo;n&rsquo;Roll alive for a new generation &mdash; and yes, some say he&rsquo;s the perfect son-in-law&hellip; until he hits the stage. 
 ​ 
 Alongside the familiar Rock&rsquo;n&rsquo;Roll show, TOMMY ROMERO also offers the Stray Cats Special &mdash; a rare tribute performed only a few times a year. Back to the roots, double bass included, bringing the Stray Cats classics authentically to the stage.
